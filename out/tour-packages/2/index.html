20 Days Dubai & Abu Dhabi Tour Package

Package Overview

Experience an unforgettable 20-day holiday in the United Arab Emirates with our Dubai & Abu Dhabi Tour Package. Explore Dubai’s modern attractions, enjoy exciting desert experiences, discover Abu Dhabi’s iconic landmarks, and have plenty of time for shopping and leisure. This package includes comfortable hotel accommodation, airport transfers, sightseeing, and travel assistance for a smooth and enjoyable trip.

Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Dubai
Depart from Islamabad and arrive in Dubai. Meet the representative at the airport and transfer to your hotel. Check-in and rest.

Day 2 – Dubai City Tour
Explore the major attractions of Dubai, including Jumeirah Beach, Burj Al Arab, Palm Jumeirah, and other famous landmarks.

Day 3 – Burj Khalifa & Dubai Mall
Visit Dubai Mall and enjoy spectacular views from Burj Khalifa. Spend the evening exploring the surrounding attractions.

Day 4 – Desert Safari
Enjoy an exciting desert safari with dune bashing, traditional entertainment, and a desert camp experience.

Day 5 – Dubai Marina & Dhow Cruise
Explore Dubai Marina and enjoy a relaxing dhow cruise with beautiful views of the city skyline.

Day 6 – Shopping & Leisure
Enjoy a free day for shopping, dining, and exploring Dubai at your own pace.

Day 7 – Old Dubai & Dubai Frame
Discover the traditional side of Dubai and visit Dubai Frame. Explore the historic areas and local markets.

Day 8 – Miracle Garden & Global Village
Visit popular seasonal attractions and enjoy an evening of entertainment, food, and shopping.

Day 9 – Leisure Day
Free day for personal activities, shopping, or optional excursions.

Day 10 – Dubai to Abu Dhabi
Transfer to Abu Dhabi and check in at the hotel. Spend the rest of the day at leisure.

Day 11 – Abu Dhabi City Tour
Explore Abu Dhabi’s major landmarks and enjoy a guided city sightseeing experience.

Day 12 – Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque
Visit the magnificent Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and explore the surrounding attractions.

Day 13 – Yas Island
Enjoy a visit to Yas Island and explore its entertainment and leisure attractions.

Day 14 – Leisure Day
Free day to relax, explore Abu Dhabi, or enjoy optional activities.

Day 15 – Optional Attractions
Enjoy optional activities such as Ferrari World or other attractions according to your preference.

Day 16 – Abu Dhabi to Dubai
Return to Dubai and check in at the hotel. Evening at leisure.

Day 17 – Shopping Day
Enjoy a full day for shopping at Dubai’s famous malls and markets.

Day 18 – Dubai Marina & JBR
Explore Dubai Marina and Jumeirah Beach Residence. Enjoy the evening at leisure.

Day 19 – Free Day
Enjoy your final day in Dubai for shopping, sightseeing, or personal activities.

Day 20 – Departure
Check out from the hotel and transfer to Dubai International Airport for your return flight to Islamabad.

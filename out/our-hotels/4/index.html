Description

Emaar Mektan Hotel, Madinah

Emaar Mektan Hotel is a budget-friendly stay in Madinah, built for pilgrims who want simple, clean rooms without paying for extras they don't need. Every room is air-conditioned, with a private bathroom and daily housekeeping.

Emaar Mektan Hotel Location

Emaar Mektan Hotel sits within easy reach of Al-Masjid an-Nabawi, with short transport options for daily visits to the Haramain. Restaurants, pharmacies, and small grocery stores are all within walking distance, which matters most for families and elderly pilgrims.

Emaar Mektan Hotel Facilities

Guests at Emaar Mektan Hotel get 24-hour reception, elevator access, daily housekeeping, and a prayer-friendly environment throughout the property. Wi-Fi is available depending on the room.

Who Emaar Mektan Hotel Suits Best

Emaar Mektan Hotel works best for families and budget-conscious groups who want to stay close to the Haramain in Madinah without paying for a luxury stay.
